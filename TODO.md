## Next

- Driving: 0-60 (14.5%) and quarter-mile medians are worse since the game's traction control, which does not act at take-off, replaced the made-up one; the standing-start clutch model is not traced. The game's stability management and traction control only act above 30 mph, so powerful old rear-drive cars can still spin at low speed.
- Flyers: the "{0}/{1} DISCOUNT SIGNS SMASHED" notification is not shown, and their mass comes from the guessed density rule.
- HUD notifications: the combined notification (HUD_COMBINED_NOTIFICATION) is not driven, because its queue and timing (around 0x8289e808) were not traced; the flyer, outpost discovery ("HORIZON OUTPOST DISCOVERED", 10 s) and barn find notifications wait on it.
- Event starts: every career event with a zone is offered, since career progression (which unlocks them) does not exist; each zone burns its flare, the `<object>_flare` SimpleEmitter of Ribbon_00/ParticleEmitters.xml, while it is enabled and the car is within 500 m (0x8298dcf0); what hides a zone's flare through 0x8298b648 was not traced, so zones are never hidden; the prompt's event icon is not chosen, so event prompts show DEFAULT: the icon table is traced (0x8289acf8, where outposts use PRHUB), and a career event's icon is its kind's base plus its Events.Level plus 1 (0x82506e18, called from 0x82507388), but the kind (0x82534cb0) and the point-to-point test (0x8253b180) were not traced.
- Speed traps: shown on the map once discovered; their "completed" state, the tutorial (Activity/SpeedCamera/TutorialRadius) and the FirstSpeedTrap100MPH award are not done, and the leaderboards are online-only. The gate trigger's box is built with VMX128 code (0x8296d1b8) not decoded, so a pass is the car's ground point crossing the line between the posts.
- Speed zones (9, `average_speed.xml`): not implemented. The zone follows the road network between its two gates, which the game builds with the same A* as the sat-nav (0x82d9fc70 runs it leg by leg; `RouteSearch` implements it with the sat-nav's costs, the zone's cost set is not traced), and its map line and its average both need that route.
- Horizon outposts: discovery, the map icon and the activation prompt work; what the zone opens (CShowGasStationScreen: fast travel for Activities/Outposts/TotalCost credits less the Discount_N_Missions share, and its three missions) needs credits, fast travel and missions.
- Car sounds: wind, suspension bottom-out, transmission whine and surface-change one-shots exist on the disc but are not played; the player's four tyres play centred.
- Cars: environment reflections are not produced, so chrome and glass reflect black: Colorado sets TrackSettings Performance/DynamicCubemap, so the game renders the environment cube around the car at run time (0x82424244, then filtered by BlitLightingCubeFaceFromCubeMap) and uses staticCarCubemap.xpr (which now decodes, world::readXprCube) only through useStaticCubeMap, set per car (0x82468810: the view's flag from 0x8246D448, or car field +632 equal to 2) by rules not fully traced. Mirrors are not produced either, nor the car drop shadow (its texture is rendered from the car with the dropShadow techniques, but the camera's scale, the opacity input and the blur kernel were not traced). The lamps' reverse, indicator and running-lamp amounts and the light-state bits (dynamicLights2.yw, dynamicLights3) have no source; tyre deformation and wheel scale are not traced, so some cars' tyres sit oversized in their arches; the body sits on the ground because physics does not apply maxdata's stock ride height.
- Track shading: the per-part ModelData (c148, set per draw at 0x82444D4C from +60 of the part record) and the per-model emissive override (+48 of the instance record, 0x82444B40) are not traced to the files, so every model uses the scene's emissive threshold with ModelData 0; the headlamp pools on the track (bEnableDeferredLightContribution, HeadLightParams1 and 2) need the deferred light buffer the game renders into the shadow mask's colour channels (0x82EA0778), which does not exist; the trees' per-draw FadeValues (0x82443850) are not set.
- Sun shadows: the game's four cached levels and three near cascades, the screen mask and its blur are drawn (ShadowPass), but only on tracks that override the light direction (Colorado does; the direction's source otherwise was not traced). Not done: the cars' SMask pass (where it is drawn was not traced), so the screen depth holds the world only; crowds and procedural vegetation cast nothing (which passes draw them was not traced); ShadowColor and StaticShadowColor, whose readers were not found; the executable's ShadowMapLevelTM builders, replaced by matrices matching this renderer's maps; the camera flag that gates the level update (0x8247ECAC). Cost at 1600x900: about 5.5 ms CPU and 6 ms GPU while driving.
- Clouds: far and close layers are drawn; the cube-map pass, cloud-set selection and transitions (the disc has one set), the elapsed-time source (taken as real time) and square-root-space blending are not done.
- Effects: tyre smoke and dust run on stand-in inputs for two wheel values the game's tyre model computes; rim smoke, backfire flames and engine smoke are not triggered. The track's 1,305 TriggerableEmitters (house and chimney smoke, leaves, birds, road dust, campfires, waterfall mist) are read but not run: their 930 TriggerZones become trigger volumes (0x82dc4640, 0x82dc3450) that switch them by id through the particle presentation (TriggerEmitter 0x82dc6658; UntriggerEmitter 0x82dc2600, presumably on leaving), and neither the volumes' subject nor their callbacks were traced. The effect's time of day gates them (0x82dbeff8).
- Stopped cars still creep slowly on steep slopes (2 m in 30 s at 20%): the tyre model has no static friction at walking speed.
- Title screen: the boot movies before it (`T10_MS_Combined.wmv`, then `Dolby_Corona_Intro.wmv`, then `forza_intro.wmv`) are not played; the profile sign-in and the signed-in SPLASH_MENU are skipped; what the idle attract (front-end request 6) plays was not traced.
- Loading screens: the initial load follows prepare_free_roam (the "freeroam" tips until streaming, cars, the radio and the initial traffic are in, then the 1 s fade from black). Fast travel and loading into a race from the menus (CLoadIntoCareerRace, festival tip groups) do not exist yet. Starting or restarting a race from free roam uses no tip screen in the game: prerace_prepare blacks out and waits for streaming and cars, and how its black-out is lifted was not traced, so that wait is not done here. Linked GL programs are cached in `~/.cache/fh1-native/shaders`.
- Pause menu: its entries for multiplayer, photo mode and my profile lead nowhere yet. The help bar's packing of its buttons by their widths (0x8289e100) is not implemented, so help buttons keep their authored widths instead of fitting their text; this applies to every screen with a help bar.
- Race AI: in autopilot races the AI regularly hits scenery at corners and loses tens of seconds, before and after the steering rewrite.
- Collision: asking once per car whether the collision mesh is the ground broke races and was reverted; world contacts still cost about 0.15 ms per car per physics step.
- Post-processing: the game's chain runs (square-root scene target, adaptive exposure, bloom with persistence, filmic day and night curves, zone-blended grading tables, desaturation matrix, top and bottom vignettes, the 16 post-processing zones and their fog). Not traced, so left out: the combine's viewport scale and offset (vignetteScaleOffsetPower.xyz, taken as the full picture), the sampler filters of the blur and add passes (taken as bilinear), the camera that switches the vignette off (0x82905050), the fade-to-colour and photo-mode terms of the colour matrix, the renderer flag that turns grading off (+0xC63), the freeze flags of the exposure adaptation, and the 10-bit quantisation of the targets. The adaptation moves 0.075 of the way per frame as the game's does, so at more than the console's frame rate it settles faster. Light rays (0x824A8870), motion blur (0x824909A0), depth of field and FXAA are not done.
- Radio in races: the game's `StartRadioFlowForEvent` (a named track timed to the start) is implemented in the radio but not called; gameplay DJ lines, positional stations and forced playlists are not played.
- Traffic: road classes and roads come from racing lines as a stand-in, because the `.nav` segments are not decoded for traffic yet.
- Smashable masses: `physicsdefinitions.bin` is not read.
- Minimap and HUD: the group route line, the mini leaderboard, text shadows, career decorations on icons and the "radio tuning" note are not shown (their data or trigger was not found).
- Full map: A sets the sat-nav's waypoint on the marker under the cursor or on the road point nearest it, and the HUD's sat-nav readout shows the distance left along the route. Missing: the icon symbolizers' own pick (0x82da2d50: their offsets and DynamicSnapSize), so markers are hovered within a radius of this engine's; the overlay's sounds (Map_RouteAdd, Map_RouteRemove, Map_RouteChangeDisallowed, Map_Snap); the player bar's class badge (hidden until its class and PI, 0x825e6518 and 0x825e6620, are traced), its texts at the last wristband (the Horizon Final query at 0x828c6dec) and the colour of its COLLECT text.
- The HUD's rev dial looks lighter in play than in the HUD tool; needs a reference from the real game.
- Race HUD: the HUD scene's lap, last-lap and best-lap timers are fed only the race time, because the rule that fills them (0x828b3f10 and the slot setters 0x828b0d70-0x828b10dc on the race status object) is not traced; the wrong-way warning (`WRONG_WAY_ON`), the position bars' gap times (`SHOW_TIME`, `TEXT_TIME`), the corner arrows (`HUD_CORNER_ARROWS`) and the end-of-race countdown (`HUD_END_OF_RACE_COUNTDOWN`) are not driven; the position bars do not show. After the finish the game shows its post-race scenes (`h_post_race_finished`, `h_post_race_results` and the rest), which are not implemented, so nothing shows the results and free roam comes back after a chosen 8 s. The start and finish gates (`anim_startfinishlinesparks.pgeo`, `obj_startline_digital_panels`) are not placed.
- Driving line: colours compare the car's speed with our own AI's speed profile, since the game's speed planner (0x824a4e40) is not traced; the route's height overrides (0x8342dd20), the stencil mask it is drawn under, the "Braking" and "Off" settings and the free-roam sat-nav line are not implemented.
- Cars whose model fails to load are not drawn (they used to be drawn as outlines).
- Road discovery: the roads driven (which colour the map's roads) are saved with the profile's next save, as in the game, which saves nothing when a road is completed; the "ROADS DRIVEN" {0}/{1} notification waits on the combined notification; the colour curve the map profile loader applies to every colour (0x82cf46b0, through 0x8247c8e0) is not worked out, so the maps use the profile's colours as written.
- Profile: `$XDG_DATA_HOME/fh1-native/profile/ForzaProfile.xml` holds the schema's properties and the game state this game has (roads driven, speed trap discoveries and bests, outposts discovered, discount signs smashed, Main/UpgradeDiscount), saved at the game's micro save requests (a speed trap best, an outpost's discovery, a smashed sign), and, by this engine's own rule with no console equivalent, once on leaving the game (QUIT, closing the window). Missing: the save container's PlayerDatabase file (cars owned, purchased parts and upgrades, event and race results in the NewProfile_* tables), whose rows are written by post-race listeners (CPostRaceUpdateProfile, 0x825858f8) not traced; the CSaveProfile moments (car change, missions, the event unlock map flow), whose flows do not exist here; the autosave indicator (CHudStatusAutosave, COMPONENT_AUTOSAVE); the micro save gate (0x824ad880); the rally expansion's properties and state tree. settings.ini's `manual` and `metric` are not tied to Main/Shifting and Options/Units: their value encodings were not traced (0x82557970 maps Units 0, 1 and 2 to -1, 0 and 2 for a call whose meaning is unknown).
