# To do

What is left before this plays like Forza Horizon, from player reports and from the code. Each item is taken from the game's own data or code, not approximated; where that is not possible yet, the item says what is missing.

## Next

- World levels of detail: chunks are drawn one by one by the game's rule (the camera's zone lists them, their distance lies in their band), and procedural copies take their own block's distances. Still missing: the zone-file models' level flags (the u32 the game copies to each draw at 0x82E192C0, whose source in the PVS draw record did not match the model names' levels), so world-space terrain, roads and buildings keep made-up bands ({0, 400, 1500, 4000} m) instead of their draw records' ranges; the field-of-view scaling of the second level's end (0x82470D18, input not traced); distances to the oriented box of zone-file props rather than their world bounds; the distance scale of procedural copies, 1 while driving but raised by a render mode (CPresentation +9519, set through CRenderThread slot 39) whose caller was not found; the procedural cross-fades and distance fade (FadeValues, see ScatterPass). Zone streaming follows the camera's zone only (0x8241FFA8); PVSZoneSpeeds.dat is loaded but its use was not traced. A disk cache of built tiles was not added: tile builds take 25 ms at the median, the world around the start streams in 2.2 s, and the full map at every level is 4.8 GB of geometry, over what a GPU budget of 3 GB allows to preload.
- Driving: 0-60 (14.5%) and quarter-mile medians are worse since the game's traction control, which does not act at take-off, replaced the made-up one; the standing-start clutch model is not traced. The game's stability management and traction control only act above 30 mph, so powerful old rear-drive cars can still spin at low speed.
- Flyers: the "{0}/{1} DISCOUNT SIGNS SMASHED" notification is not shown, and their mass comes from the guessed density rule.
- HUD notifications: the combined notification (HUD_COMBINED_NOTIFICATION) is not driven, because its queue and timing (around 0x8289e808) were not traced; the flyer, outpost discovery ("HORIZON OUTPOST DISCOVERED", 10 s) and barn find notifications wait on it.
- Event starts: every career event with a zone is offered, since career progression (which unlocks them) does not exist; each zone burns its flare, the `<object>_flare` SimpleEmitter of Ribbon_00/ParticleEmitters.xml, while it is enabled and the car is within 500 m (0x8298dcf0); what hides a zone's flare through 0x8298b648 was not traced, so zones are never hidden; the prompt's event icon is not chosen, so event prompts show DEFAULT: the icon table is traced (0x8289acf8, where outposts use PRHUB), and a career event's icon is its kind's base plus its Events.Level plus 1 (0x82506e18, called from 0x82507388), but the kind (0x82534cb0) and the point-to-point test (0x8253b180) were not traced.
- Speed traps: shown on the map once discovered; their "completed" state, the tutorial (Activity/SpeedCamera/TutorialRadius) and the FirstSpeedTrap100MPH award are not done, and the leaderboards are online-only. The gate trigger's box is built with VMX128 code (0x8296d1b8) not decoded, so a pass is the car's ground point crossing the line between the posts.
- Speed zones (9, `average_speed.xml`): not implemented. The zone follows the road network between its two gates, which the game builds with the same A* as the sat-nav (0x82d9fc70 runs it leg by leg; `RouteSearch` implements it with the sat-nav's costs, the zone's cost set is not traced), and its map line and its average both need that route.
- Horizon outposts: discovery, the map icon and the activation prompt work; what the zone opens (CShowGasStationScreen: fast travel for Activities/Outposts/TotalCost credits less the Discount_N_Missions share, and its three missions) needs credits, fast travel and missions.
- Car sounds: wind, suspension bottom-out, transmission whine and surface-change one-shots exist on the disc but are not played; the player's four tyres play centred.
- Cars: the dynamic environment cube (EnvironmentCube) is rendered as the game does (256 texels, all six faces a frame under Horizon_Race's EnvMapFrequencyScale, centred 0.5 m above the player's car, mips through the lat-long map) and every car reflects it with reflectionScaler 1. Not done, their rules not traced: the face list's cap of MeshLimit meshes ranked by size over distance (0x82470E00; its scale factors at +180/+184 of the list descriptor were not found), VisibilityThreshold (0x82CEEAE8), the buckets drawn only while the sun shadows are on, procedural copies, cars and particles in the faces (0x82428F40 and the object list at +80 of the renderer were not followed), the clouds' cube-map pass, where cubeSkySaturation and cubeSkyScale are read, the camera queries that force one face a frame (vtable slots 116-124), the renderer flags +9519/+9520 (3x FarDistance) and +8316, and car field +632 of the useStaticCubeMap rule. The IBL cubes (BlitLightingCubeFaceFromCubeMap) are off in the game's own descriptor. Cost at 1600x900 while driving: about 1.3 ms CPU and 2 ms GPU (one layered pass; drivers without GL_ARB_shader_viewport_layer_array draw face by face, about 5 ms CPU). Mirrors are not produced either, nor the car drop shadow (its texture is rendered from the car with the dropShadow techniques, but the camera's scale, the opacity input and the blur kernel were not traced). The lamps' reverse, indicator and running-lamp amounts and the light-state bits (dynamicLights2.yw, dynamicLights3) have no source; tyre deformation and wheel scale are not traced, so some cars' tyres sit oversized in their arches; the body sits on the ground because physics does not apply maxdata's stock ride height.
- Track shading: the per-part ModelData (c148, set per draw at 0x82444D4C from +60 of the part record) and the per-model emissive override (+48 of the instance record, 0x82444B40) are not traced to the files, so every model uses the scene's emissive threshold with ModelData 0; the headlamp pools on the track (bEnableDeferredLightContribution, HeadLightParams1 and 2) need the deferred light buffer the game renders into the shadow mask's colour channels (0x82EA0778), which does not exist; the trees' per-draw FadeValues (0x82443850) are not set.
- Crowds: the people of the track's crowd sets are drawn as the game's CProceduralCharacters draws them (CrowdRenderer, spectators/CrowdDraw.h): every person of a shown set as a sprite, fading in from 40 to 60 m and out from 650 to 700 m, and the first 500 queued within 80 m (walkers within 200 m) as skinned models by distance bands with caps, their clips stepping a frame per 30th of a second of the presentation clock; each person's model, clip, frame and size come from the set's generator as the init draws them. Not traced, so left out or inferred: which sets show in free roam and in a race (CrowdSession's rule is read from the sets' tags and routes; the game's gate was not found); the pixel shaders' c360_0 albedo factor (the tint squared times a factor whose writer was not found, plus the ambient), taken as 1; which draw pass is the main view's (the sprite fade pair that meets the models' fade is taken); the sprite quad's CamLook convention (taken as the view direction); the headlight term; the photo camera's zoom scaling; the camera-flash effects of the crowd (0x82EB9E80); crowd sun shadows (the depth passes' shadow use was not traced; the game has no blob shadows for these crowds); the clip cross-fade the shader supports but the prepare step never sets. The runtime's rand() that picks cheers is shared with the whole game, so its sequence is not the game's. Cost at 1600x900 while driving: about 0.15 ms CPU and 0.15 ms GPU.
- Sun shadows: the game's four cached levels and three near cascades, the screen mask and its blur are drawn (ShadowPass), but only on tracks that override the light direction (Colorado does; the direction's source otherwise was not traced). Not done: the cars' SMask pass (where it is drawn was not traced), so the screen depth holds the world only; crowds and procedural vegetation cast nothing (which passes draw them was not traced); ShadowColor and StaticShadowColor, whose readers were not found; the executable's ShadowMapLevelTM builders, replaced by matrices matching this renderer's maps; the camera flag that gates the level update (0x8247ECAC). Cost at 1600x900: about 5.5 ms CPU and 6 ms GPU while driving.
- Clouds: far and close layers are drawn; the cube-map pass (so the environment cube shows no clouds), cloud-set selection and transitions (the disc has one set), the elapsed-time source (taken as real time) and square-root-space blending are not done.
- Effects: tyre smoke and dust run on stand-in inputs for two wheel values the game's tyre model computes; rim smoke, backfire flames and engine smoke are not triggered.
